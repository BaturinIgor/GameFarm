Support

Found a bug, lost a purchase or have an idea? Write to zdrgvretrsyhtrse@mail.ru. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually answer within two days.

Restoring purchases: open the Shop and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Ads and tracking: rewarded videos are optional. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
