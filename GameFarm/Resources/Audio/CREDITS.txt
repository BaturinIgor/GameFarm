GameFarm audio credits
======================

All source audio is public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal). Attribution is not required but is given here with thanks.

Sources
-------
- Kenney audio packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0: https://kenney.nl
    - Impact Sounds:    https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
    - RPG Audio:        https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
    - Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Ninja Adventure - Asset Pack by Pixel-boy (music and sounds), CC0 1.0:
    https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack

Processing: converted to AAC .m4a (SFX and ambience mono 64 kbps, music stereo 96 kbps, 44.1 kHz),
leading silence and silent tails trimmed, SFX level-matched (peak / short-term loudness),
music loudness-matched to about -18 LUFS. Ambience loops were trimmed and given a crossfade at the loop point.
Files marked "mix" were layered from the listed sources. amb_fire is synthesized (no source file).
